Another one begins where the last one thinned,
a quiet echo beneath the skin—
not louder, not brighter, not trying to be,
just *another one*, unfolding gently.

It borrows a rhythm from something before,
a half-open window, an unlatched door,
steps in softly, unsure of its name,
carrying fragments that feel the same.

But listen—closer—there’s something new:
a shift in the light, a different hue,
a pause that lingers a second too long,
a note slightly bent in a familiar song.

Another one isn’t a copy or twin,
it’s where we repeat and subtly begin—
a quiet insistence that change can hide
in the smallest turn of an inward tide.

So let it arrive without needing a crown,
no need to lift it or weigh it down.
For every “another” we think we know
is a place where something else can grow.
